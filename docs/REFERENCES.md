# Lucerna References

This document records the sources used for Lucerna's Rosary prayers, Scripture passages, guidance, fruits, reflections, artwork, and rights.

Quoted text is reproduced as published by the cited source. Lucerna's short guidance lines are original prompts based on the practices listed below.

The app keeps these reference records offline. Its external links let readers check the original pages when a network connection is available.

## Rosary text

Lucerna uses the traditional English prayer forms published by the Dominican Rosary Center.

| Prayer                           | Source                                |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Sign of the Cross                | Rosary Center, How to Pray the Rosary |
| The Apostles' Creed              | Rosary Center, How to Pray the Rosary |
| The Our Father                   | Rosary Center, Prayers of the Rosary  |
| The Hail Mary                    | Rosary Center, Prayers of the Rosary  |
| The Glory Be                     | Rosary Center, Prayers of the Rosary  |
| O My Jesus, or the Fatima Prayer | Rosary Center, Prayers of the Rosary  |
| Hail Holy Queen                  | Rosary Center, Prayers of the Rosary  |
| Final Prayer                     | Rosary Center, Prayers of the Rosary  |

The mystery is announced on the large bead used for the decade's Our Father. The ten Hail Marys follow on the ten smaller beads.

The Glory Be and optional Fatima Prayer follow the decade on the cord. This placement follows the linked traditional diagram and the cited prayer order.

## Guidance

The following lines are Lucerna's own concise prompts. Each line remains tied to the practice that supports it.

| Prayer step              | Guidance                                                                           | Source basis                                                                          |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First opening Hail Mary  | Pray this Hail Mary for faith.                                                     | Rosary Center: three Hail Marys for faith, hope, and charity                          |
| Second opening Hail Mary | Pray this Hail Mary for hope.                                                      | Rosary Center: three Hail Marys for faith, hope, and charity                          |
| Third opening Hail Mary  | Pray this Hail Mary for charity.                                                   | Rosary Center: three Hail Marys for faith, hope, and charity                          |
| Mystery announcement     | Look upon the scene, hear the word, and pause in silence before the decade begins. | `Rosarium Virginis Mariae`, sections 29, 30, and 31; Holy See per-decade instructions |
| Decade Our Father        | Lift your mind to the Father as the decade begins.                                 | `Rosarium Virginis Mariae`, section 32                                                |
| Decade Hail Marys        | Meditate on the mystery while praying these ten Hail Marys.                        | USCCB, step 7                                                                         |
| Decade Glory Be          | Crown the decade with praise of the Trinity.                                       | `Rosarium Virginis Mariae`, section 34                                                |
| Fatima Prayer            | Often added after each decade, as asked by Our Lady at Fatima.                     | USCCB note after step 7                                                               |
| Hail Holy Queen          | The five decades complete, the Rosary turns to Mary.                               | USCCB, step 8                                                                         |
| Final Prayer             | Ask to imitate what the mysteries contain and obtain what they promise.            | Rosary Center Final Prayer and USCCB concluding sequence                              |
| Fruit of the Mystery     | The fruit appears with its mystery.                                                | `Rosarium Virginis Mariae`, section 35; USCCB fruit list                              |

## Rights

The Douay-Rheims Bible text is public domain. The traditional prayers are public-domain liturgical text.

The cited Vatican and USCCB pages support the selected mysteries, fruits, prayer order, and guidance. Lucerna does not bundle those web pages.

The bundled edition of `The Secret of the Rosary` is the 1954 United States edition translated by Mary Barbour. No matching renewal appears in the Stanford Copyright Renewal Database.

The Met records identify their bundled images as CC0. Wikimedia Commons records identify the remaining files as public domain or PD-Art.

An artwork's underlying work, digital file, and source record can have separate rights statements. Lucerna retains the source statement attached to each file.
